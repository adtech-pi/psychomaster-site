A note before we start

This quiz asks you to reflect on how you respond to stress, conflict, and emotionally charged situations. The questions are based on real patterns that trauma researchers and psychologists recognize.

What this is: A tool for self-reflection and psychoeducation. It can help you recognize patterns you've always sensed but never had words for.

What this is not: A clinical diagnosis, a substitute for therapy, or a fixed identity. Your responses developed in specific contexts to keep you safe — they're not flaws.

Some questions may feel familiar; others may not resonate at all. That's okay. There are no wrong answers here — only honest reflection.
