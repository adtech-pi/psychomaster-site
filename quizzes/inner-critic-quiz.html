Here's What This Quiz Is

Your inner critic is the voice you've been carrying — usually absorbed early, from caregivers, culture, or systems that taught you to be hard on yourself.

It's not your enemy. It's a protective mechanism that went overboard. Understanding it is the first step to responding to it.

There's no "bad" result. Every critic type has a reason, and every one has a path toward a gentler inner voice.
