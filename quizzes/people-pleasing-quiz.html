A Note Before You Begin

This quiz explores patterns around people-pleasing — the ways we adapt ourselves to meet others' expectations, sometimes at the cost of our own well-being.

Some questions describe situations that may bring up feelings of guilt, anxiety, or discomfort. If exploring these patterns feels timely and supportive for you right now, we encourage you to continue.

If you're currently in a difficult period or dealing with significant distress, this may not be the right moment. That is perfectly okay. Come back when it feels right.

You can stop the quiz at any time. Your progress is saved locally and you can return to it.
