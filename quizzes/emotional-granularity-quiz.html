    A Quick Note Before You Begin

    Emotional content ahead. This quiz asks you to reflect on your emotional experiences. If you're going through a particularly difficult time or experiencing overwhelming emotions, this may not be the right moment. There's no pressure to take it now — you can always come back later.

    This quiz is a psychoeducational tool, not a clinical assessment or diagnosis. Results reflect your current emotional awareness patterns — and these can shift over time with practice and support.

    Privacy Policy

    This quiz is designed with your privacy in mind. All your responses stay on your device — nothing is sent to any server.

    Data stored: Your quiz progress is saved in your browser's local storage so you can pause and resume. This data never leaves your device.

    No tracking: We don't use analytics, cookies, or third-party trackers on this page.

    Share cards: The canvas-generated share card is created entirely in your browser. No image data is uploaded anywhere.

    If you'd like to clear your saved progress, you can do so from your browser's settings or by refreshing the page without answering.

    Looking for More Support?

    This quiz is a starting point for reflection — not a substitute for professional care.

    If you're finding that difficulty identifying or experiencing emotions is affecting your daily life, speaking with a licensed therapist can be genuinely helpful. Therapists trained in emotional processing, alexithymia, or somatic experiencing can offer tailored support.

    Need to talk to someone?

    If you're in a crisis, please contact a mental health professional or a crisis helpline in your area immediately.
