Before You Begin

The HSP Navigator Quiz is a self-reflection tool, not a clinical diagnosis. It is grounded in Dr. Elaine Aron's Sensory Processing Sensitivity (SPS) research.

There are no right or wrong answers. Answer as honestly as you can, without trying to figure out what the "best" answer is.

Take your time. You can always go back to previous questions.
