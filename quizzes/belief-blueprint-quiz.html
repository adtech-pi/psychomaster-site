There's a voice inside you.

Not the one that narrates your day — a deeper one. It speaks in certainties: I'm not enough. I can't trust anyone. I have to earn love.

These aren't random thoughts. They're beliefs. And they have a history.

Core beliefs are the underground rules you formed in childhood about how the world works and who you are. They're invisible — you've lived with them so long they feel like facts. But beliefs can be changed. And the first step is knowing which one is running the show.
