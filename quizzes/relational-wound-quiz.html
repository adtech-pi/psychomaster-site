Before your first memory was fully formed...

Your nervous system was already learning something profound about relationships — not through words, but through the tone in your caregiver's voice, the consistency of their presence, the way they responded when you needed them most.

These early experiences carved a groove — a core relational wound — that your adult relationships keep falling into, over and over again, without your permission.

This quiz will help you identify that wound — not to label you, but to illuminate the pattern so you can finally stop repeating it.
